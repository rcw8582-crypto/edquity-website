## Practical notes

Send these individually, not as a group mail with eight addresses in it. These
families shared a disability file with you, and a visible recipient list would
be a confidentiality problem as well as a trust problem.

Chase once, after a week, with one line and the link again. Do not chase twice.
With eight families you are looking for four or five responses, and the phone
call offer will convert more of them than a second email will.

Take the calls. With a group this small, fifteen minutes on the phone teaches
you more than the form does, and a transcript gives you sentences you can quote
to a funder that no checkbox ever will. Ask permission to take notes, and ask
at the end whether you may quote them anonymously.
